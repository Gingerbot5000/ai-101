03 / Education Proof

What the evidence actually says

The best research does not say “AI is magic.” It says outcomes improve when the tool, the method, and the human setup are aligned.

Meta-analysis signal
0.86

Recent education syntheses still point to meaningful upside.

Chatbot and generative-AI studies can climb above 1.0 in some settings, but the stronger read is that design and scaffolding change the result.

Source: recent education meta-analyses summarized for this workshop.
Harvard physics trial
4.5 vs 3.5

The Harvard physics trial showed a pedagogically tuned AI tutor outperforming the same students’ classroom condition on post-test mastery.

Human-AI hybrid
Stanford Tutor CoPilot

Stanford Tutor CoPilot improved student mastery most for less-experienced tutors, suggesting AI can raise the floor when humans stay in the loop.

Source: Harvard and Stanford tutoring studies.
Operational takeaway

structured use helps. unstructured use can backfire.

Prompting, tutoring, and workflow gains are strongest when the activity is clearly staged and the human still owns the judgment. Remove the productive struggle entirely, and later performance can sag.

Technique is part of the outcome.

09 / Safety

Use it boldly, use it responsibly

A good AI workflow keeps momentum high without pretending the tool is infallible.

Protect sensitive data

Do not paste passwords, SSNs, or confidential records into consumer chat tools. Business and API products often have different privacy defaults.

Verify important claims

Dates, statistics, citations, and legal or medical guidance still need human checking before you trust them.

Disclose meaningful AI help

Be honest when AI shaped work you are presenting professionally, academically, or publicly. Trust is worth keeping.

Human review is a feature

Use AI to accelerate thinking, not replace it. The strongest results still come from human judgment plus AI leverage.

Respect ownership and IP

Know what content you are allowed to reuse, remix, or publish. Fast output is not the same as clean rights.

Don’t normalize misuse

Deception, impersonation, and lazy misinformation scale just as fast as the good stuff. That part is a choice.
