## Notes

**Top 3 for Maximum Audience Impact**
- **#4 Interview Coach** — participatory, personal, instantly relatable; ask for a volunteer.
- **#1 Resume Rewrite** — high before/after contrast; use a real audience member's bullet with permission.
- **#3 ELI10** — humor lands every time; layered explanation shows AI range.

**Risk Flags**
- Demo #11 requires facilitator improvisation; skip if presenter isn't comfortable ad-libbing.
- Demo #8 requires a real document snippet — prepare a printed excerpt as a prop.
- Demo #4 at Risk=3 because audience responses vary; always have the STAR fallback ready.

**Sequencing Suggestion**
Lead with #3 (low stakes, funny) ? #1 or #4 (personal/emotional hook) ? #5 or #10 (entrepreneurial) ? close with #12 (builds trust, addresses fear).

**Offline Safety Net**
Pre-run every setup prompt and save responses as screenshots. If Wi-Fi fails, flip to the screenshot deck — audience sees the same output without live dependency.
