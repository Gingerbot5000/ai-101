## Usage Notes for AI-101 Deck

- **Overlays:** Add 40–60% dark overlay on hero photos for text legibility.
- **Consistency:** Pick 1–2 sources and stick to them per deck to maintain visual coherence.
- **Color treatment:** Desaturate stock photos and add brand color tints for unified palette.
- **Illustrations vs. photos:** Mix unDraw/Humaaans for conceptual slides, real photos for "real world" application slides.
- **Avoid:** Any photo showing branded AI products (ChatGPT logo, Gemini UI) — crop or substitute with generic interfaces.
